# About Me

I am a sophomore to junior level engineering student majoring in Mechanical engineering at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte. I attain an eager mind that strives to absorb as much knowledge as I can find. If there is certain areas where I lack within the field of engineering, I always make it a personal admiration of mine to become familiar with what it is that I am lacking. Over my first few years if college, I have developed skills in Excel, presentations, CAD, SolidWorks, Fusion360, Creo Parametric, 3D printing and many more. However, my mind is still starving to become as skilled as I can in these fields. I was brought to Mechanical engineering for how truly challenging it is. It does not get much harder than this. It takes pure grit and determination to learn to deal with problems in a way that others deem impossible. However, I am determined. I aim to be the engineer that will be faced with a problem and solve it, even if I have not before. That is what I work towards each and every day.

"What does it mean to defend an engineering decision : and do you currently know how to do it?"

My belief is that engineering is all about perspective. Being an engineer is about taking a given situation at what is seemingly face value and finding a new viewpoint that defies what everyone believed to be true. So with that being said, defending a decision is about trusting in what you have developed within yourself. When someone else who may be "smarter" than you tells you that your view is wrong, your decision is wrong, it is up to you to trust that your engineering mind is right. Engineers are not perfect humans, however, they learn to think in a way that no other human is prone to think. Am I capable of this? Not to my fullest extent, no. I have soi much more I can learn and so much I can grow from before I can look at my engineering decisions and say, "I am right."

Over the course of this whole assignment with all the problem-solving and revisions I did, it took well over five hours to complete. It was not easy one for me in the slightest.
